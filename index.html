03 / CANDLE PSYCHOLOGY

Read the behavior
behind the candle.

Candles do not predict the future by themselves. They record the struggle between acceptance, rejection and urgency.

REJECTION WICK

Price visited. The market refused.

Long upper wick: buyers pushed upward, sellers absorbed or overwhelmed them, and late buyers may now be trapped.

Ask: was meaningful liquidity or structure tested?
ENGULFING

Control shifted quickly.

A strong body overruns the previous range. Useful only when location and context support the story.

Ask: what did the expansion invalidate?
INSIDE BAR

Compression before commitment.

Range contracts. Both sides are waiting. The psychological mistake is predicting direction before evidence arrives.

Ask: what would confirm expansion?
IMBALANCE
DISPLACEMENT

Urgency entered the auction.

A large impulsive candle can reveal aggressive repricing. Chasing it can create poor location even when direction is correct.

Ask: can I wait for structure instead of emotion?
